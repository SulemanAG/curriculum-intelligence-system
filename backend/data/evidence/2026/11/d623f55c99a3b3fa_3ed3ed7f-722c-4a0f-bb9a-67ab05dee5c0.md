# ACM Computer Science Curricula 2023 (Test Fixture)

Undergraduate computer science programs must emphasize foundational systems principles, software security, and distributed computing.

## Distributed Consensus and Fault Tolerance

Students must acquire rigorous theoretical and practical competencies in consensus algorithms (such as Raft and Paxos), failure models, and data consistency models including linearizability and eventual consistency.

## Agentic AI Systems Integration

Curricula should prepare engineers to build multi-agent systems with explicit tool grounding, deterministic human gates, and auditable evidence provenance.
